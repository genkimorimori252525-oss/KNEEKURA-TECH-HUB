# KNEEKURA TECH HUB Constitution v1.0

This document defines non-negotiable safety and knowledge-quality rules for the Hub.

## Identity Rule
Knowledge Entity IDs are immutable and never reused. Merged or retired IDs remain resolvable through history or redirects.

## Evidence Rule
A curated claim that represents a direct observation, author statement, or experiment result must be anchored to identifiable evidence and a source snapshot or stable source locator.

## Inference Rule
An inference never becomes a direct observation merely because an AI, summary, or later record repeats it.

## Merge Rule
Canonical Knowledge Entities are never merged automatically. Automation may only propose duplicate candidates.

## Promotion Rule
AI alone cannot promote a claim to `VALIDATED`.

## Independence Rule
Evidence with unknown lineage is not counted as independent evidence.

## Score Rule
The Hub does not expose a single global quality score for a technology. Assessments must preserve evidence, applicability, costs, risks, unknowns, and counterevidence.

## License Rule
License detection and content-handling permission are separate decisions. Unknown licensing never implies permission to copy full source.

## Freshness Rule
Upstream change does not erase historical evidence. It may make current-use claims stale or trigger re-verification.

## Deletion Rule
Canonical knowledge is not hard-deleted by automation. Retire, merge, reject, challenge, or supersede while keeping an audit trail.

## Discovery Rule
Discovery may be broad and inexpensive. Promotion into curated knowledge is deliberately more expensive.

## Authority Rule
The Hub supplies decision material; it is not itself proof that a design should be adopted.
