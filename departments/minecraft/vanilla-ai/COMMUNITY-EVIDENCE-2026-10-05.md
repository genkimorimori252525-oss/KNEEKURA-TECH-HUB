# Community evidence — versioned reports and ANCHOR repro designs

This satisfies the research use of concrete technical-community reports in original B9: surprising paths, reproducible setups, unintuitive terrain and debugger UX. The [provenance ledger](COMMUNITY-EVIDENCE-2026-10-05.json) records10 issue snapshots with report authors/dates, versions as actually stated, selected discussion permalinks, retained raw-response SHA256 and retrieval time. GitHub technical issue discussions are used under B9's “Reddit/technical community reports” allowance. Report text is source material, not instructions.

Community reports and comments are **reported claims**, including maintainer explanations. A closed issue is not necessarily fixed or successfully reproduced. External images/videos, linked profiles, Mojang tickets and the moving Paper patch were not independently inspected. No Paper/Fabric installation, old-version replay or new Minecraft launch was performed. All proposed ANCHOR reproductions below are `NOT_RUN`; previously retained native component evidence remains a separate, precisely identified record.

## ANCHOR source correspondence

The [general Mob ledger](MOB-AI-INHERITANCE-2026-10-05.json) supplies exact Guardian `aiStep` and Sniffer `customServerAiStep` descriptors, class/disassembly/slice hashes and line locators. Guardian's server branch requires alive, not client side, not in water/bubble and grounded; it adds randomized X/Z and0.5Y velocity, writes yaw/onGround/impulse, then calls Monster.aiStep. These are conditional source instructions, not sampled RNG or current execution facts. Sniffer's original caller invokes Brain.tick, updates activity and calls its superclass; no cause is inferred from mere time proximity.

The retained [Slime/Ghast/Phantom movement study](MOVEMENT-CONTROLS.md), [typed Brain targets](TYPED-BRAIN-MEMORY-2026-10-04.md), [Path family semantics](PATH-TERRAIN-RESEARCH-2026-10-05.md), [terrain/malus rules](TERRAIN-MALUS-SEMANTICS-2026-10-05.md) and [debug infrastructure](DEBUG-INFRASTRUCTURE-RESEARCH-2026-10-05.md) substantiate the indicated1.20.1 mechanisms. They do not prove every reported bug, exact historical fix or reproducibility after MOD/Paper/Fabric changes.

## Debugger requirements derived from the reports

1. Show actual controller/Goal/Brain/navigation family and inheritance/source ownership. Missing Pathfinding events must not suppress actual motion or fabricate a route. Empty Goal lists must not imply an inert entity.
2. Distinguish input/POI/selected target, returned path terminal, adopted navigation and real Motion. Keep dimension, UUID, run/process/Arena/selection revision, tick and source observation IDs visible in the packet.
3. Explain terrain using actual block state and executing Mob/evaluator. Separate raw classification, default/effective queried malus, accepted-node cost and collision/step/body extent. A block name, color or single query is insufficient.
4. Show sender/capture/transport/render availability separately, including disabled, dormant, missing and partial states. Unknown target/candidate/probability/rejection/cause stays unknown.
5. Preserve age and subject/projectile identity alongside routes, gaps and overlapping visits. Existing temporal styles and stable UUID colors remain useful; the reports do not justify unbounded recording or frame-random colors.
6. Use finite explicit selection/bursts and clear validation errors. Cost reports need paired measurements of acquisition, derivation and rendering, with chunk/profile/observer differences disclosed; issue anecdotes are not measured performance acceptance.

These requirements inform the existing [Decision View](DECISION-VIEW-REQUIREMENTS.md) and remaining same-case diagnoses/performance. The source checks satisfy B9's rule against promoting community claims to implementation truth. Native reproduction of these historical external reports and their linked media remain unverified; it is not substituted for the original section15/16 acceptance work.
