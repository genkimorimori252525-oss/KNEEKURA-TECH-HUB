# Stopped-parent Motion retention

The user's report prompted static inspection of the native cache/geometry/renderer and the retained Viewer. No inspected path clears selected Mob history merely because velocity becomes zero or Navigation stops. Native geometry ages actual sample timestamps through NEW/MIDDLE/OLD and expires them at100tick; removal of a related projectile ends acquisition without deleting its retained samples. The Viewer builds traces from retained tracks independently of current entity positions. A selection revision, run/Arena boundary, invalid source order, disabled overlay or raw-capture suppression has a separate explicit effect.

One premature-loss case was reproduced: three related projectiles move, then remain stationary. Their completed-tick observations continue to consume the single global128-position display budget. The unchanged code evicts the travelled endpoints before their100tick lifetime, so the movement line disappears although its timestamps have not expired. This confirms a possible cause, rather than identifying the user's exact observed surface/entity without further context.

The related-projectile display cache now coalesces an interior stationary sample only when three successive retained positions are exactly equal in coordinates/dimension/class, both adjacent tick intervals are at most10 and no explicit gap is removed. The actual first endpoint and latest completed-tick endpoint remain; every retained point still cites a real observation ID. Raw/canonical evidence is unchanged. No synthetic point, interpolation or refreshed movement timestamp is introduced. Omitted intermediate display samples can still produce an honest retained SOURCE_GAP; they never connect a missing/dimension boundary. The16-group/global128-position and geometry bounds remain in force. Many moving projectiles can still exhaust that finite budget; this change does not promise an unlimited100tick history under arbitrary load.

The regression failed on the unchanged cache and passes after the fix: three stationary projectiles retain unexpired movement, their latest endpoint is actual tick150, and the movement expires by its original endpoint age. Parent removal retains eligible geometry; resumption uses the last actual endpoint. Source gaps and dimension changes remain visible. The selected-Mob test also proves that stationary observations preserve the old movement until expiry. The existing moving16-projectile test still exercises global-cap eviction.
