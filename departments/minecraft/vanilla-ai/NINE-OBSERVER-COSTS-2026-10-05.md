## Observer effect and remaining attribution limits

CPU boundary windows and requested10second wall windows differ and are both retained. CPU totals cover all game/server/client/render/writer threads and OS/JFR query overhead. Same saved bytes do not force equal RNG, scheduling, ready-time ticks or JIT state. Browser/CAS/retained-evidence checks overlapped some early trials, so the host is not a controlled benchmark machine. Differences can reverse sign; no causal overhead or equivalence claim follows.

Burst event/byte budgets may end before the measurement window. Event-build timers omit final encoding/writer and do not automatically include every once-per-process source proof. Snapshot and query cold maxima are retained rather than discarded. Perturbative deep modes remain opt-in diagnostic capture, not an always-on gameplay authority.

OS GPU utilization is sampled at two process-engine boundaries; it is not draw duration. [Earlier actual Tank framebuffer/pacing evidence](TANK-DISPLAY-AND-PACING-2026-10-05.md), browser projected pixels, native overlay submit counters and GPU completion are separate proof classes. GPU completion/overlay-only GPU attribution remain unmeasured. The default-OFF, finite storage/cache, explicit eviction/drop, no unbounded world scan, no visualization gameplay objects and unchanged original search-call contracts remain enforced by source/genuine tests.
