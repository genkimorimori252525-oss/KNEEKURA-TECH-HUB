## Paired A/B and correctness gates

- **REDSTONE-BFS-LEDGER**: vanilla vs AC 1.7.0 (same server/world/seed), compare settled POWER and observed sequence of piston/observer notifications, redstone item duplication (issue #27), 1/100/10000 wires, MS precision.
- **LIGHT-CHUNK-BOUNDARY**: vanilla vs exact Starlight 1.1.2, check 3D light map at boundaries and block/sky sources, chunk unload and relay; Create/Framed isolated; collect threads and lock graph on hang.
- **CHUNKGEN-PALETTE-INVARIANTS**: Noisium v2.3.0 disabled/enabled in stable newly generated chunks (and Canary separately), compare fluid/nonempty/tick counts and hash of palette output after save/reopen.
- **XP-CONSERVATION**: Clumps installed/uninstalled, XP map distribution saved+reloaded, mending repair ratios, BloodMagic event and canceled pickups; check number of live orbs and pickup p99 spikes.
- Require corresponding exact user JAR bytes/loader and dependency closure, compile/remap, reproducible oracle, disposal/cleanup and permissions. **No GameTests performed and no measured performance.**
